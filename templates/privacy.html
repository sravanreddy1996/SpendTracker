Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expense records you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the Spendly service to you — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling filters. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We apply industry-standard measures to protect it from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and are never readable by anyone, including us.

You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and database services) to operate the app; these providers process data only as necessary to deliver the service and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to access your data, or would like to request deletion, please reach out to us. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Your privacy matters to us. We will keep this policy updated as the service evolves.
